Why you keep falling back into it
(Or why nothing ever sticks)

Your lack of change isn’t because you can’t do it, it’s because your identity and your beliefs are protecting something else.

Holding back is actually your mind’s way of being super intelligent. It’s keeping you safe.

When your identity is built on old beliefs, fear or shame, it prioritises staying safe over growth, discipline and lasting change.

It’s not that you don’t want to change; your mind simply can’t commit to it while it’s busy defending the old version of you.

When the foundations are cracked, decorating the house is pointless. Every few months the walls come down and you blame the paint.

IntroducingChosen Man

Chosen Man is my groundbreaking program for men who feel stuck, that focuses on identity and discipline.

It is designed to help you break your loops, drop the masks, keep your word to yourself, have real conversations and experience an explosion of confidence and drive, all while rebuilding your identity from the root up.

You will see changes within weeks if you go all in with my process.

This program is broken down into two powerful phases.

Phase one is a three month LIVE.

In the first three months, we dive deep. This is designed to give you everything you need to fully understand and implement what works for you.

We’ll focus on:

  • Identifying and addressing exactly what is causing the loops and self-sabotage in your life.
  • Equipping you with all the knowledge, tools and strategies to create fast and lasting progress.
  • Providing intensive, hands on support so you know exactly what to do to transform the way you live.

The goal of this phase is to create drastic results in a short amount of time.

After the LIVE phase, you’ll have the remaining three months to continue receiving my support daily.

This will ensure you have:

  • Ongoing guidance for any challenges that come up for you
  • Tailored solutions as you grow and progress
  • Steady support to keep the momentum going and to celebrate every breakthrough (as there will be plenty).

This program is designed to create transformative results whilst also giving you the support you need to sustain them.

How will this work? What is included?

The “Chosen Man” program is not just a course, it’s a fully immersive, results driven experience designed to create real change in your life.

Here is how it’ll look:

Phase one: The 3 Month LIVE

During the 3 months LIVE period, a new class is released each week that you can watch on-demand over the weekend. The classes are structured teaching designed to create breakthroughs in your identity and habits.

We will meet as a group once a week on Zoom. These two hour live sessions are where I answer questions, provide personalised coaching and solve specific problems you’re facing.

On top of the group calls, you also get 1-1 calls with me. Most coaches only ever do group calls. I go further, because I will always make time for the men who choose to invest in their growth. That’s also why enrollment is only open for 9 days and spots are limited.

If you can’t attend live it’s no problem. All calls are recorded so you can access them at any time.

In between calls, you’ll have access to written Q&A in a private group chat. This is an intimate, safe space for men who are in a similar situation to you. Any questions or issues that arise between calls, this is the space to go and be supported.

You also get direct WhatsApp access to me. Not a support team, not an assistant. Me.

My approach is very personalised to where you are in your life right now. This ensures it’ll be exactly what you need to move the needle forward.

There's a lot of hands-on support and accountability. This is less like a course you take and more like an initiation where I am by your side so that you will transform in the best of ways. We’ll be on this journey together.

Phase two: Aftercare

While the first 3 months are just going to prepare you for everything you need. Change doesn't stop there. You'll continue to have direct access to myself, my team and all the resources for an extra 3 months, so you can continue to implement what you've learned, while being fully supported.

The first three months is powerful. But real change happens when you continue to move the needle, and watch yourself change before your eyes.

After three months, you're going to know the exact plan. You're going to know everything you need to do. You're gonna know exactly what's important and what's not.

You will have access to monthly coaching calls to checkin with me and optional support calls, where you can get personalised help. You can use these as needed. You can be as hands-on or as hands-off as you want.

You will continue to have access to the private chat with the small group of men you started the journey with. This is where you can ask written Q&A questions and get my support in between live calls. You'll have this for the whole 6 months period.

So much will change in the first 3 months. And after that you can just keep going and tap back in whenever you've got questions or changes or adjustments or needs help.

It's so much easier to move forward with progress when you are not doing this alone and always have a safe space to tap into whenever you need.

This is a group program with 1-1 support built in, which most programs don't offer. You get private 1-1 calls with me, and on the weekly live calls I run hot seats where I coach you directly on exactly what you're facing, in front of men who are dealing with the same things.

You'll also be split into a small accountability pod of men matched by age and situation, so the conversations stay relevant to you. Between calls, you can post in the private chat or message me directly on WhatsApp.

Most men find the group makes the work faster, not slower, because hearing another man say out loud what you've never admitted is often the moment it shifts for you. And if you pay in full, you also get an extra private 1:1 session with me on top of everything else.

Are you a therapist? What makes you qualified?

I'm not a therapist. But I consistently get results with men that years of courses, podcasts and self-help content haven't given them, because I go to the root instead of the routine.

I grew up on a council estate with two alcoholic parents, barely speaking English, stuttering, and getting picked on for it. My mum died when I was eight. The last thing she told me was to never settle for less and to keep my standards high, no matter what anyone says. I went into foster care at eleven, and from then on I taught myself everything, from YouTube videos and ancient texts, because nobody was coming to do it for me.

I built Chosen Man because I was the man watching other people's videos wondering what was actually stopping me. It wasn't the estate or the money or the stutter. It was what was running underneath. Once I went for that, everything moved.

I did this on myself from the worst starting position you can get. Since then I've done it with over [100] men, many of them older than me, who had tried everything else first.

To be clear, this is coaching, not therapy. If you're dealing with a mental health crisis, please speak to your GP or a qualified professional first. The Initiation can work alongside that, not instead of it. Don't take my word for any of this. Watch the replay and hear it from the men themselves.
